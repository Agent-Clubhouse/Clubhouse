## Disposition awaiting independent re-review

F1: raw `append(accepted)` is rejected. Main can capture an opaque volatile
capability only for the original live writer process/attempt; it must be marked
entered once at the actual sink after the shared generation fence. A dedicated
ACK method checks it and its coverage digest. A genuine late same-process ACK
after handoff_unknown may confirm only that original generation and increments
`lateAcknowledgementCount`. Duplicate ACK publication is idempotent. Recovered
uncertainty cannot mint a capability, use another writer's handle, or confirm
from caller-created accepted metadata. Already committed ACK replay is durable
prior evidence, not recovery inventing a callback. Main receipt-sink honesty is
an integration invariant, not a claim to authenticate providers from this pure
store. Neither a receipt capability process ID nor member generation allocates
the separately owned runtime/session generation.

F2: complete checksum-damaged and zero-filled final frames **remain refused**,
with original bytes unchanged and no trimming. This is a deliberate conservative
storage-integrity/liveness tradeoff requiring visible explicit recovery; manual
board reads/healthy shards remain available. The suggested conservative repair
barrier is deferred until its coverage/final-boundary/interrupted-recovery proof
exists. Incomplete uncommitted append-tail recovery is a separate existing
scope, not arbitrary physical/post-fsync corruption recovery. Contract text now
states this limitation instead of implying the stream always resumes.

F3: `excludedSequenceCount` counts board sequence positions including non-post
changes. It is not an excluded-message count. Validated checkpoint counters and
tests use that name. Control-character validation also refuses DEL.

F4: at the50 eligible-post cap, `through` must equal max supplied eligible seq,
or the scan is rejected without advancing state. Smaller lists still require
the trusted board owner to establish complete-range provenance. The truncated
50-of100 negative and exact50 positive are tested. No automatic source scan or
notification owner has been wired yet.
