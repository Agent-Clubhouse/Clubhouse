## Verdict

**CHANGES REQUESTED (narrow, one finding).** The full-read fix is correct and well tested. The **role Connections page** path now pops requested rows under byte pressure, which breaks that page's "every requested record remains represented" contract.

## Correct

- **Budget handling** (`remote-agent-details.ts:50-55`):
  - `budget = min(options.maxBytes ?? MAX, MAX)`, so the default and the cap are both clamped;
  - validation comes **after** `authorized()`: an invalid budget (0, -1, 1.5, NaN, ∞) gives 500 `details_budget_invalid`, and an unauthorized caller still gets 403 first.
- **Paths using the budget:** the full-read trimming loop (:312) and the inventory-page guard (:119).
- **Role caller** (`selected-dispatcher-details.ts`): the overhead is the byte length of `JSON.stringify({version, role, agentId, details: null})` minus `'null'`. That is the actual UTF-8 size including escaping (tests use `'rôle "quoted" \\ 名前'`). The budget passed is `MAX - overhead`, and the existing outer guard (wrapper > MAX gives 500) is retained.
- **Unchanged:** auth and receipt behaviour. The two annex-server test changes are call-shape only.
- **Inventory-page gap:** not reachable for the role read. The role route accepts only Connections queries, and orchestrate gives 400 for a Dispatcher, so no extra proof is needed there.

## Finding M1-a: role Connections page rows popped under the budget

- **How the page is built:** for a Connections page, `selectedConnectionPageEnvelope(result)` (`remote-details-presentation.ts:31-51`) empties the other cards. It then **truncates text** to fit `REMOTE_DETAILS_MAX_BYTES` (the hard-coded MAX, not the budget) and adds the notice "Every selected record and field remains represented".
- **What goes wrong:** control then reaches the generic loop at :312 with `budget = MAX - overhead`. If the envelope fits MAX but not the budget (a window of about the wrapper overhead, roughly 60–100+ bytes), the loop **pops rows from the largest card, which is the requested Connections card**. It increments `omitted`, so requested records are silently dropped.
  - That contradicts the page contract from PG1/#14: requested rows are never popped for byte pressure, and text is truncated instead.
  - The new test ("applies the budget to a Connections page") only checks an exact fit, which needs no trimming, and an irreducible budget of 64 bytes. It doesn't cover this window.
- **Fix:**
  - pass the budget into `selectedConnectionPageEnvelope(result, budget)`, so text truncation targets the budget and `details_page_limit` is thrown against the budget;
  - keep the generic pop loop from removing rows of a page-only requested card.
  - Add a control: a role Connections page whose envelope lies between the budget and MAX keeps **all requested rows**, has its text truncated with the notice, and the role response fits within MAX.
  - The mutation should be that the envelope ignores the budget, which then fails the control.
