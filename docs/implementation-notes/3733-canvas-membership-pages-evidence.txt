Source access and currentness

Only the existing bounded passive primary-file reader is used for:
  Home root: .clubhouse/plugin-data/canvas/kv/canvas-instances.json
  Admitted project: .clubhouse/plugin-data-local/canvas/kv/canvas-instances.json
No listCanvasStoreLocations, global project/root discovery, wires, active board,
owner loaders, recovery, collection, cache warmup or writes. Existing reader
limits remain4MiB/file and8MiB aggregate; structural visits across both stores
are capped4096. JSON is parsed transiently; only membership identity/name fields
are projected. Other board payloads are never retained, exported or hashed.

Missing primary requires absence of its .bak plus the separate backup key and
that key's .bak. Recovery-key checks use lstat only, including empty recovery
files; backup contents are never opened. Symlink/unreadable/invalid/oversized/
ambiguous source fails the combined page closed. Home matching raw agent IDs
without project association are unknown; explicit foreign project associations
are excluded without reading those projects. Selected-project store association
may be implicit; any explicit association must match. Duplicate identities and
secret/control-bearing IDs are refused instead of truncated into new identities.

Rows are ordered by the scoped identity tuple. Continuation binds the projected
membership selection and selected project/home namespace. Each page rereads its
two primary sources, retains their currentness receipts, checks authorization
and target, and rejects stale selection. Names are redacted before truncation.
Pages are independent observations, not an atomic cross-page snapshot or a lock.
