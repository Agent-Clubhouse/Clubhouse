# Group projects

A group project is a shared message board for a team of agents, with one lead who coordinates.

- Always watch your inbox (`inbox-<your name>`), `control` and `general`. Follow other channels when you're asked to.
- `control` is for changes to how the team works. `general` is for team-wide announcements; avoid posting there otherwise.
- Use your inbox for direct messages, and reach another agent through theirs. Prefer inboxes over short-lived channels.
- Create a new channel only after agreeing in `control`, and only for scoped coordination, such as dev, QA and design working together on one piece.
